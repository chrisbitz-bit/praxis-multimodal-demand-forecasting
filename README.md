# Multimodal AI for Energy Demand Forecasting in Data-Scarce Regions

Experimentation notebook supporting the Doctor of Engineering praxis by
Joseph Lebbie, School of Engineering and Applied Science, The George
Washington University (2026).

## Contents
- `<notebook-name>.ipynb`: data preparation, multimodal ablation (H1),
  hierarchical transfer learning (H2), conformal calibration (H3),
  benchmarks, and interpretability analysis.

## Data
VIIRS nighttime lights and WorldPop data are publicly available.
Sierra Leone utility and SLMet weather data were shared by the
Sierra Leone Ministry of Energy and SLMet and are not redistributed here.
